Debug Tools Now Available

May 15, 2025

We've enhanced our support capabilities with new troubleshooting tools that will help us resolve issues more quickly. If you encounter a problem and contact support, our team may ask you to:

  1. Open the left sidebar menu
  2. Scroll down to the "Debug Tools" section
  3. Click on "Debug Menu"
  4. Share specific information from the screens that appear

This new capability allows our support team to diagnose problems faster and provide you with better assistance, especially for complex location-based or calendar display issues.
