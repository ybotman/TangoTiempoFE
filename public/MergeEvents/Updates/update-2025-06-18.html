Improved Event Creation Experience

June 18, 2025

✨ What's New

Creating events just got easier! We've added smart validation to help you create complete, engaging events:

  • Required Field Validation - Clear indication of what information is needed before saving
  • Helpful Reminders - Gentle suggestion to add an event image for better visibility
  • Save Without Image Option - Flexibility to create events quickly and add images later

🎯 Why This Matters

No more guessing why your event won't save! The new validation dialog clearly shows what's missing and helps ensure your events have all the essential details attendees need.
